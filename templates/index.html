Algorithms Used

TF-IDF Vectorizer

Term Frequency-Inverse Document Frequency converts sentences into mathematical vectors, highlighting important words while reducing the weight of common words.

Cosine Similarity

Calculates the angle between two sentence vectors to find out how similar they are. If the angle is small, the sentences share similar meaning.

TextRank (PageRank)

Builds a graph where sentences are nodes and similarity is edges. It uses Google's PageRank algorithm to find the most central and important sentences.

NLP Techniques Used

Tokenization

Breaking down the large text paragraph into individual sentences and tokens (words) for easier processing.

Stop-word Removal

Filtering out common words like "is", "the", "and" which don't carry much meaning, to focus on important keywords.

Extractive Summarization

Selecting the most important existing sentences from the original document rather than generating new text.
